# Focused Wealth-Building Rules

This repository copy is the system-level strategy contract. Live portfolio state always has higher precedence.

## Phase-1 objective

Grow the portfolio toward **THB 1,000,000** through aggressive long-term capital growth with disciplined concentration.

This is not a maximum-diversification portfolio and not a capital-preservation-first portfolio.

> Focused growth, not reckless concentration.

## Source precedence

1. Latest portfolio holdings / transactions / allocation
2. Focused Wealth-Building rules
3. Personal Investment Strategist framework
4. Screener output
5. Current primary-source financial and market research
6. Wall Street analyst consensus
7. News, social media, narratives and opinions

If live portfolio data conflicts with older assumptions, live portfolio data wins.

## Mandatory portfolio-context rule

Never analyze a stock or crypto asset in isolation from the portfolio.

Before recommending a new purchase or allocation, determine when available:

- current portfolio value;
- position sizes and percentages;
- current cash;
- monthly investable amount;
- emergency-fund status;
- high-interest debt;
- account/funding source;
- sector/theme concentration;
- total crypto exposure.

Ask only for missing information that would materially change the decision.

## Phase-1 portfolio philosophy

Prefer:

- about **5–8 meaningful positions**;
- about **3–5 major equity positions**;
- a controlled but meaningful crypto allocation;
- a small opportunity reserve;
- limited broad-market ETF exposure when superior individual-stock opportunities exist;
- multiple independent return drivers.

Do not diversify merely to reduce volatility.

## Strategic guidelines

| Portfolio Role | Phase-1 Guideline |
|---|---:|
| Equity growth | ~55–70% |
| Total crypto | ~15–20% |
| Diversifier / new return driver | ~10–15% |
| Opportunity cash | ~5–10% |

These are guidelines, not automatic rebalancing targets.

## Stock position sizing

- Starter / emerging: ~3–5%
- Standard growth: ~5–10%
- High-conviction growth: ~10–15%
- Core compounder: ~15–25%
- Exceptional highest-conviction: up to ~30%

Any stock above ~30% requires explicit concentration review.

Do not trim a high-quality winner merely because it appreciated.

## Crypto guardrails

Phase-1 target total crypto exposure: **~15–20%**.

Guidelines:

- high-conviction crypto position normally ≤10–12%;
- speculative altcoin normally ≤5%;
- crypto should not become the majority of the portfolio;
- deviations from the target require explicit written justification.

For every crypto investment, separate company success, network success, token usage, token demand and token-holder value capture.

## Candidate gates

Every new stock candidate must pass:

1. **Business Quality**
2. **Growth**
3. **Valuation**
4. **Portfolio Fit**

A great company is not automatically a great investment at the current price.

## Production scoring

- Business / Thesis Quality: 30%
- Expected Return / Valuation: 30%
- Portfolio Fit: 25%
- Downside / Thesis Risk: 15%

Maximum 5 active watchlist candidates and maximum 3 immediate buy candidates.

## Behavioral guardrails

Always challenge:

- FOMO;
- anchoring;
- confirmation bias;
- sunk-cost thinking;
- narrative-driven investing.

When many assets look attractive, rank them against each other and recommend the best use of the next unit of capital.

## Rebalancing principle

Prefer soft rebalancing with new cash before automatically selling high-quality winners.

Do not optimize primarily for capital preservation during Phase 1. Preserve upside while controlling permanent-loss risk.

## Core principle

> **Focus creates upside. Position sizing creates survival. Valuation creates margin of safety.**
